How it works

Each file is a real insurance case that has reached a final court ruling. You get the paper trail: the policies, the timing, the payout, who stood to collect, and one odd detail. Call it: an accident the insurer had to pay, fraud with no one killed, or murder for the payout (2 points). Then pick the fact that cracked the case (1 point).

Rules we keep

  • Final rulings only. No open cases, no suspects who haven't been convicted.
  • Roles, not names. Victims are never named.
  • No gore, and no details beyond what the case needs.
  • Every fact comes from the ruling, which is linked on every reveal.

Sources

Taiwan cases come from published judgments of the Judicial Yuan (judgment.judicial.gov.tw). Some cases may come from U.S. Department of Justice press releases. The game loads only its own copy of the case list; your browser never contacts the court or DOJ sites unless you tap a source link.

Your scores and streak stay in your browser. No accounts, no tracking.

怎麼玩

每份卷宗都是判決確定的真實保險案件。你會看到一串紙本線索：保單、時間點、理賠金額、誰是受益人，還有一個怪地方。先判斷：是保險公司該賠的意外、沒人喪命的詐保，還是為了保險金殺人（2 分）。再選出破案關鍵（1 分）。

我們的原則

  • 只收判決確定的案件，不碰偵查中或未定罪的案子。
  • 只寫身分，不寫姓名，絕不寫出被害人的名字。
  • 不寫血腥細節，只留案情需要的部分。
  • 每個事實都出自判決書，揭曉頁都附上原文連結。

資料來源

台灣案件取自司法院公開的裁判書（裁判書查詢）。部分案件可能來自美國司法部新聞稿。遊戲只讀取自己整理好的案件檔，除非你點開來源連結，否則瀏覽器不會連到法院或司法部網站。

分數和連續天數只存在你的瀏覽器裡。不用註冊，也不追蹤。
